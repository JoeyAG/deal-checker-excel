Deal Checker for Excel

This page is hosting the Deal Checker panel for Excel. Download your manifest (the small file that tells Excel where the panel lives), then follow the steps in the setup guide.

The manifest points at

What to do with it

  1. Save the downloaded deal-checker-manifest.xml into a folder on your PC, for example C:\DealCheckerAddin.
  2. Share that folder with yourself, then add it to Excel's Trusted Add-in Catalogs.
  3. Restart Excel and add Deal Checker from Home → Add-ins → More Add-ins → Shared Folder.

The setup guide has every click.
